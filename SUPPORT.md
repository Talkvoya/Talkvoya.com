# Talkvoya support

[Home](README.md) · [简体中文](SUPPORT.zh-CN.md)

- **Installation or setup:** Read the [Harness setup guide](TalkvoyaHarness/HELP.en.md).
- **Reproducible issue or product idea:** Use the [GitHub issue forms](https://github.com/Talkvoya/Talkvoya.com/issues/new/choose). Include the app version, Android version, device model, and reproducible steps. Remove keys, personal data, and chat content from logs or screenshots.
- **Security and privacy vulnerabilities:** Follow the [private reporting policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Official contact:** Visit [Talkvoya.com](https://talkvoya.com/) or open **Settings → About → Contact us** in Harness.

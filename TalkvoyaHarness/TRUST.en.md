## What runs locally

The app runs its bundled tools on your device. API keys and chat records are stored on the device. When you send a model request, relevant input, including tool context needed for the task, may go to your selected provider. That provider’s terms and privacy policy apply to its processing and billing.

## Phone permissions

Phone control is optional and uses Android Accessibility when enabled. The app may use screen context to perform a task. Review permission mode and your sensitive-app list before running tasks involving payments or private files. You can stop a task and turn the service off in Android Settings.
